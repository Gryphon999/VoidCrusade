## Voice-over

The recorded voice lines in `public/voice/` were synthesised with Microsoft Edge online text-to-speech (via the `edge-tts` tool). That service is meant for personal use; before any commercial release (paid sales or ad revenue) re-record the pack with a commercially licensed engine (Azure Speech or ElevenLabs on a paid plan): `npm run voice -- --force`.
